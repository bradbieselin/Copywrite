# LinkedIn Post Templates

> **Purpose:** Templates for Brad's LinkedIn content — the primary channel for thought leadership, lead generation, and brand building. Covers multiple post formats and content pillars.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad for daily/weekly LinkedIn posting

## Contents

### Post Formats

#### 1. The Teardown Post
- Structure: "I analyzed [brand]'s [content type]. Here's what they're doing right and what I'd change."
- Hook → brand intro → 3 positives → 3 improvements → takeaway
- Length: 150-250 words
- Engagement driver: people love seeing real examples dissected

#### 2. The Framework Post
- Structure: "Here's the exact [framework/process/formula] I use to [achieve result]."
- Hook → context → step-by-step framework → application example → CTA
- Length: 150-200 words
- Engagement driver: actionable, saveable content

#### 3. The Contrarian Take
- Structure: "Unpopular opinion: [common practice] is wrong. Here's why."
- Hot take → reasoning → evidence → alternative approach → discussion prompt
- Length: 100-150 words
- Engagement driver: controversy and debate

#### 4. The Story Post
- Structure: personal narrative that teaches a lesson
- Hook → situation → conflict → resolution → lesson → application
- Length: 150-250 words
- Engagement driver: emotional connection and relatability

#### 5. The Results Post
- Structure: "We did [thing] for a client. Here's what happened."
- Hook with numbers → context → what we did → results → key takeaway
- Length: 100-175 words
- Engagement driver: proof and social proof

### Weekly Schedule
- Monday: Framework or educational post
- Wednesday: Teardown or results post
- Friday: Story or contrarian take

### Hashtag Strategy
- 3-5 hashtags per post
- Mix of: #DTCmarketing #ecommerce #copywriting #emailmarketing + niche tags
