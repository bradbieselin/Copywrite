# Content Pillars

> **Purpose:** Defines CopyDTC's 4-5 core content themes that guide all of our own marketing — LinkedIn posts, Twitter threads, blog content, lead magnets, and speaking topics.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad (and future marketing team) for all CopyDTC marketing content creation

## Contents

### Pillar 1: DTC Copy That Converts
- Focus: tactical copywriting tips, formulas, and frameworks specific to DTC ecommerce
- Examples: email subject line formulas, landing page structure breakdowns, ad copy hooks
- Goal: establish expertise and provide immediate value

### Pillar 2: AI-Powered Content Production
- Focus: how to use AI (specifically Claude) to produce professional content at scale
- Examples: workflow walkthroughs, prompt engineering tips, quality control processes
- Goal: differentiate CopyDTC as AI-forward, address "isn't AI content bad?" objections

### Pillar 3: Brand Voice & Strategy
- Focus: building a brand voice, maintaining consistency, voice as a competitive advantage
- Examples: brand voice teardowns, "this vs that" comparisons, voice development process
- Goal: position CopyDTC as strategic partners, not just content factories

### Pillar 4: DTC Growth Insights
- Focus: broader ecommerce and DTC trends, marketing channel strategy, growth playbooks
- Examples: email marketing benchmarks, social media trends, ad platform changes
- Goal: show we understand the whole business, not just the copy

### Pillar 5: Behind the Agency
- Focus: building an agency, lessons learned, transparency about our process and business
- Examples: revenue updates, client wins, mistakes we made, hiring/scaling decisions
- Goal: build personal brand and trust through authenticity

### Content Mix
- 40% Pillar 1 (core expertise)
- 20% Pillar 2 (differentiation)
- 15% Pillar 3 (strategy positioning)
- 15% Pillar 4 (thought leadership)
- 10% Pillar 5 (personality and trust)
