# Weekly Content Generator Prompt

> **Purpose:** Claude prompt that generates a full week of CopyDTC marketing content across all channels — LinkedIn, Twitter, and any other active platforms. Input the week's theme and get back ready-to-post content.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad for weekly content planning and creation

## Contents

### The Prompt
- Input: this week's content theme or topic (tied to content pillars)
- Input: any recent wins, insights, or events to incorporate
- Input: any specific CTAs for this week (newsletter signup, DM offer, website link)

### Output
- 3 LinkedIn posts (Mon/Wed/Fri) with hooks, body copy, hashtags
- 5 tweets per day (Mon-Fri) following the daily mix formula
- 1 Twitter thread on the weekly theme
- 1 newsletter topic outline (if running a newsletter)
- Content repurposing suggestions: how to turn the week's best content into other formats

### Content Principles
- Every post should provide standalone value (not just tease)
- Mix educational, entertaining, and promotional (60/30/10 ratio)
- Reference real examples and specific numbers wherever possible
- Write in Brad's personal voice — direct, knowledgeable, DTC-focused, slightly opinionated

### Quality Bar
- Every piece should be ready to post with minimal editing
- Hooks should stop the scroll
- CTAs should feel natural, not forced
- No repetitive phrasing across the week's content
