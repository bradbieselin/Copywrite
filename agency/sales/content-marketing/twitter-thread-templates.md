# Twitter Thread Templates

> **Purpose:** Templates for Brad's Twitter/X threads — long-form content broken into tweet-sized pieces for maximum engagement and reach. Threads are CopyDTC's primary Twitter lead generation tool.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad for weekly Twitter threads

## Contents

### Thread Formats

#### 1. The Playbook Thread (8-12 tweets)
- Structure: complete process or methodology broken into steps
- Tweet 1: bold claim + "Here's the playbook:" → steps → summary → CTA
- Example: "I write 50+ emails per week for DTC brands. Here's my exact process:"

#### 2. The Mistakes Thread (8-10 tweets)
- Structure: common mistakes with corrections
- Tweet 1: "X mistakes I see DTC brands making with their [content type]:" → mistakes with fixes → CTA
- Example: "7 email mistakes killing your DTC brand's revenue (and what to do instead):"

#### 3. The Breakdown Thread (6-8 tweets)
- Structure: detailed analysis of one specific example
- Tweet 1: "I just analyzed [brand]'s [content]. It's [good/brilliant/terrible]. Here's why:" → analysis → lessons → CTA
- Example: "Glossier's welcome email is a masterclass. Let me show you why (thread):"

#### 4. The Tool/Resource Thread (8-12 tweets)
- Structure: curated list of tools, resources, or examples
- Tweet 1: "[X] resources every DTC copywriter needs:" → list with commentary → CTA
- High save and bookmark potential

### Thread Best Practices
- Tweet 1 must stand alone as compelling content
- Each tweet should make sense independently (people see individual tweets in feeds)
- End with a CTA: follow, DM for resource, link to newsletter/website
- Self-reply to add the thread to keep formatting clean
- Post threads Tuesday-Thursday 8-10am EST for maximum reach
