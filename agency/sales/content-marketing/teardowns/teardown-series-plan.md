# Teardown Series Plan

> **Purpose:** Editorial calendar and strategy for CopyDTC's recurring teardown content series. Teardowns are our highest-performing content type and primary top-of-funnel driver.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad for planning weekly/biweekly teardown content

## Contents

### Series Concept
- "Copy Teardown" — weekly analysis of real DTC brand content
- Rotating focus: emails one week, landing pages next, ads next, social next
- Mix of well-known brands (for reach) and emerging brands (for niche appeal)

### Content Calendar
- Week 1: Email teardown (welcome sequence or promotional email)
- Week 2: Landing page teardown (product page or homepage)
- Week 3: Ad teardown (Meta or Google ad)
- Week 4: Social media teardown (Instagram or TikTok content)

### Brand Selection Criteria
- Must be a real DTC brand (not B2B or SaaS)
- Preferably well-known enough that the audience recognizes them
- Must have publicly available content to analyze
- Mix of excellent and mediocre examples (don't only tear down bad content)

### Distribution Strategy
- Primary: LinkedIn post + Twitter thread (same teardown, different format)
- Secondary: newsletter deep-dive, blog post (expanded version)
- Tertiary: Instagram carousel (visual before/after)

### Lead Generation Integration
- Every teardown ends with a soft CTA ("Want us to tear down your [content type]? DM me.")
- Prospects who engage with teardowns get added to the outreach pipeline
- Teardowns of industries/niches matching current prospects get sent directly as value-adds
