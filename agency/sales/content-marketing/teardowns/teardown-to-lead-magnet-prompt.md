# Teardown to Lead Magnet Prompt

> **Purpose:** Claude prompt that transforms a collection of teardowns into a polished lead magnet (PDF guide, ebook, or email course) for capturing email subscribers and generating leads.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad when compiling teardowns into lead generation assets

## Contents

### The Prompt
- Input: 5-10 completed teardowns from the teardown series
- Input: target audience for the lead magnet
- Input: desired format (PDF guide, 5-day email course, or ebook chapter)

### Output Options

#### Option A: PDF Guide
- "The DTC Copy Playbook: [X] Lessons from Analyzing [Y] Brands"
- Structure: introduction → teardown summaries → key patterns → actionable checklist → CopyDTC CTA
- Length: 15-25 pages, designed for quick reading

#### Option B: Email Course
- 5-day email course, one teardown per day with a lesson
- Day 1: Email copy → Day 2: Landing pages → Day 3: Ads → Day 4: Social → Day 5: Putting it all together
- Each email: 300-500 words, one key takeaway, one action item

#### Option C: Compilation Blog Post
- "What We Learned Analyzing [X] DTC Brands' [Content Type]"
- Long-form SEO content (2,000-3,000 words)
- Patterns, best practices, and anti-patterns from the collection

### Quality Bar
- Lead magnet should provide genuine, immediate value
- Not a sales pitch disguised as a guide — real insights that stand on their own
- Professional design notes included for PDF formatting
- CTA at the end should feel like a natural next step, not a hard sell
