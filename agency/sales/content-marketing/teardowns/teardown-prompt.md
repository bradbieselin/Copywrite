# Teardown Prompt

> **Purpose:** Claude prompt that generates a detailed content teardown of a DTC brand's marketing. Used for both CopyDTC marketing content (LinkedIn posts, threads) and as a sales tool to demonstrate expertise to prospects.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad for creating teardown content and prospect-specific teardowns

## Contents

### The Prompt
- Input: brand name, website URL, and the specific content to tear down (email, landing page, ad, etc.)
- Input: screenshot or text of the content being analyzed
- Input: format (LinkedIn post, Twitter thread, full blog post, or prospect-specific PDF)

### Output Sections
1. **Brand Context** — 2 sentences on who they are and what they sell
2. **What They're Doing Well** — 3-5 specific positive observations with explanations
3. **What Could Be Better** — 3-5 specific improvement opportunities with concrete suggestions
4. **The Fix** — for each improvement, show a rewritten version or specific recommendation
5. **Key Takeaway** — 1-2 sentences distilling the main lesson for the audience

### Tone Rules
- Respectful and constructive — never mean-spirited
- Specific — "the headline buries the benefit" not "the headline is bad"
- Educational — teach the audience why something works or doesn't
- Balanced — always find genuine positives, even in weak content

### Format Options
- LinkedIn post (200-300 words)
- Twitter thread (8-10 tweets)
- Blog post (800-1,200 words)
- Prospect teardown (1-2 pages, more detailed, includes CopyDTC CTA)
