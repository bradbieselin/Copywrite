## Key Conversion Principles Applied Across All Samples

### Email Subject Lines
- **Pattern:** Specificity + emotion or curiosity
- **Tested Formula:** "[Action/Result] + [Specific Detail]" or "[Problem] + [Implied Solution]"
- **Open Rate Benchmarks:**
  - Skincare: 38-44%
  - Supplements: 38-42%
  - Fashion: 36-41%

### Ad Copy Structure
1. **Hook** (1-2 sentences): Curiosity or problem statement
2. **Problem Amplification** (2-3 sentences): Why existing solutions fail
3. **Solution Introduction** (1-2 sentences): How this brand differs
4. **Proof** (3-5 bullets): Specific benefits with data/testimonials
5. **CTA** (1 sentence): Clear action with stakes

### Product Page Optimization
- Lead with problem, not product
- Use before/after or before/with
- Specific numbers/metrics (not vague claims)
- Address objections upfront
- Multiple CTAs (hero, mid-page, bottom)
- Trust elements: guarantees, testimonials, transparency

### Social Proof Strategy
- Real customer names + results (not generic testimonials)
- Specific metrics (not "customers love it")
- Before/after photos or documented results
- Celebrity/expert endorsement where applicable
