# Outreach Sequence C: Warm Referral / Intro

> **Purpose:** Sequence for when you have a mutual connection or referral.
> **Emails:** 2 emails over 5 days
> **Target:** DTC brands referred to you by existing clients, partners, or network contacts

---

## Email 1: The Warm Intro (Day 1)

**Subject lines:**
- [REFERRER] suggested I reach out
- [REFERRER] thought we should connect
- Quick intro via [REFERRER]

**Body:**

Hey [FIRST_NAME],

[REFERRER] mentioned you might be looking for help with [BRAND]'s [content / emails / ads / copy]. I run CopyDTC, and we build AI-powered content systems for DTC brands.

We recently [SPECIFIC RESULT for referrer or similar client — e.g. "helped [REFERRER'S BRAND] increase their email revenue by 28% in 6 weeks by rebuilding their welcome and abandoned cart flows"].

I'd love to take a quick look at [BRAND]'s content and put together a free audit showing where the biggest opportunities are. Takes me about 30 minutes to produce, and it's yours to keep whether we work together or not.

Worth a quick call this week?

Brad
CopyDTC

---

## Email 2: The Follow-Up (Day 5)

**Subject lines:**
- Re: [REFERRER]'s intro
- Following up on [REFERRER]'s suggestion
- Quick bump

**Body:**

Hey [FIRST_NAME],

Just bumping this up in case it got buried. [REFERRER] thought we'd be a good fit, and after looking at [BRAND]'s [website / emails / social], I agree.

I noticed [ONE SPECIFIC THING — quick observation about their content]. Happy to put together a full audit if you're interested.

Would a 15-minute call work sometime this week? I can share the audit live and walk you through the findings.

Brad

---

## Key Rules

1. **Only use this when you have a real referral.** Don't fake mutual connections.
2. **Name the referrer in the subject line.** It's the single highest-impact thing for open rates.
3. **Be specific about results.** Referrals trust numbers. Give them concrete results from your work with the referrer.
4. **Offer the audit call, not a sales call.** Frame it as "I'll walk you through the findings" not "let me pitch you."
