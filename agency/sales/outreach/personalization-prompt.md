# Personalization Prompt

> **Purpose:** Claude prompt that takes a prospect's website, social media, and ad presence and generates personalized outreach angles, specific observations, and customized email copy for each outreach sequence.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad before sending outreach to any new prospect

## Contents

### The Prompt
- Input: prospect's website URL, Instagram handle, any ads found in Ad Library
- Input: which outreach sequence to personalize (A, B, or C)
- Input: prospect's industry/niche and estimated revenue range

### Output Sections
1. **Brand Snapshot** — 3-4 sentences summarizing who they are, what they sell, and how they position themselves
2. **Content Audit Quick Hits** — 3 specific, actionable observations about their current content (positive and negative)
3. **Personalization Hooks** — 5 specific details to weave into outreach emails (product names, recent campaigns, brand voice observations)
4. **Customized Email Copy** — personalized versions of all emails in the selected sequence
5. **Objection Predictions** — 2-3 likely objections this prospect will have and how to address them
6. **Recommended Approach** — which angle will resonate most and why

### Quality Bar
- Personalization should be specific enough that the prospect knows you did your homework
- Observations should be genuinely useful, not just flattery
- Customized emails should feel hand-written, not templated
