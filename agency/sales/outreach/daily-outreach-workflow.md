# Daily Outreach Workflow

> **Purpose:** Step-by-step daily routine for prospecting and outreach. Designed to be completed in 60-90 minutes per day, generating a consistent pipeline of new leads.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad (and future sales team members) as a daily habit

## Contents

### Daily Targets
- 10 new prospects researched and added to tracker
- 10 personalized first-touch emails sent
- All follow-ups sent per sequence schedule
- Responses handled within 4 hours

### Morning Routine (60-90 minutes)
1. **Check responses** (10 min) — respond to any replies from yesterday
2. **Send follow-ups** (15 min) — all scheduled follow-ups per sequence timelines
3. **Research new prospects** (20 min) — find 10 new brands that fit our ICP
4. **Personalize** (20 min) — run personalization prompt for new prospects
5. **Send first-touch emails** (15 min) — send personalized Email 1s

### Prospect Research Sources
- Shopify store directories and rankings
- Facebook Ad Library (brands actively spending on ads)
- Instagram hashtag exploration (#dtcbrand, #ecommerce, etc.)
- Product Hunt, IndieHackers for newer brands
- Industry newsletters and "brands to watch" lists
- Competitor customer lists and social followers

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- DTC ecommerce brand
- Revenue: $500K - $10M annually
- Active on at least 2 marketing channels
- Currently producing content (even if it's mediocre)
- Has a team of 2-20 people (big enough to pay, small enough to not have in-house writers)

### Tracking & Metrics
- Update prospect tracker daily
- Weekly pipeline review (Friday)
- Monthly outreach performance analysis
