# Outreach Sequence B: Value-First (Give Before You Ask)

> **Purpose:** Alternative approach for warmer prospects or brands you've already interacted with on social.
> **Emails:** 3 emails over 7 days
> **Target:** DTC brands you've engaged with on social media or whose content you genuinely follow

---

## Email 1: The Gift (Day 1)

**Subject lines:**
- Wrote this for [BRAND] (no strings attached)
- 3 subject lines for [BRAND]'s next email drop
- Free headlines for [BRAND]

**Body:**

Hey [FIRST_NAME],

I'm a fan of [BRAND] and I write content for DTC brands for a living. I had some ideas for your [emails / ads / product pages] and figured I'd just write them instead of talking about it.

Here are [3 email subject lines / 3 ad headlines / a rewritten product description] for [SPECIFIC PRODUCT]:

1. [OPTION 1]
2. [OPTION 2]
3. [OPTION 3]

Use them, don't use them, totally up to you. Just thought they might help.

Brad
CopyDTC

---

## Email 2: The Context (Day 4)

**Subject lines:**
- Why those [headlines / subject lines] work
- The thinking behind those [headlines / subject lines]
- Quick context on those ideas

**Body:**

Hey [FIRST_NAME],

Wanted to share the thinking behind those [subject lines / headlines] I sent.

[OPTION 1] works because [reason — e.g. "it creates curiosity without being clickbait. DTC email open rates jump 15-20% when you use a question format that implies a benefit."]

[OPTION 2] takes a different angle: [reason — e.g. "leading with social proof. 'X customers can't be wrong' hooks are performing really well in the supplement space right now."]

If you test them, I'd love to hear how they perform. And if you want more where those came from, I do this full-time for DTC brands.

Brad

---

## Email 3: The Offer (Day 7)

**Subject lines:**
- What we do for brands like [BRAND]
- Could help [BRAND] with this full-time
- Quick question

**Body:**

Hey [FIRST_NAME],

Last note from me. If you liked those [subject lines / headlines], here's what working together looks like:

We build AI-powered content systems for DTC brands. That means your emails, ads, product pages, and social content all running on a consistent brand voice engine. We handle the full production so your team can focus on product and growth.

Most brands start with a 30-day pilot at $3,000. We rebuild one major content channel from scratch and show you measurable results before you commit to anything longer.

Worth a 15-minute call?

Brad
CopyDTC

---

## Key Rules

1. **The free sample must be genuinely good.** This only works if what you give away is better than what they're currently running.
2. **Use Claude to generate the samples.** Load their brand voice from their website/socials, then generate options.
3. **Pick a specific product or campaign.** Don't be vague. Write for their best-seller or their latest launch.
4. **This sequence works best with social warmth.** Comment on their posts, share their content, then send this.
