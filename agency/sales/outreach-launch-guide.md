## WEEK 1: SETUP & FIRST OUTREACH

### Monday: Prepare Your Tools & Assets

**1. Set Up Email Sending Infrastructure (Choose One)**

Option A: **Instantly.ai** (Recommended for automation)
- Cost: $40/month
- Setup: 15 minutes
- Best for: High-volume outreach, follow-up sequences
- Steps:
  1. Create account at instantly.ai
  2. Connect Gmail account (uses your actual Gmail)
  3. Create email template with [FIRST_NAME], [COMPANY] variables
  4. Upload CSV of prospects

- Cost: $48/month
- Setup: 20 minutes
- Best for: Higher open rates, more compliance
- Steps:
  1. Create account
  2. Add your domain (optional but recommended)
  3. Warm up domain gradually
  4. Build outreach sequences
  5. Import prospect list

Option C: **Manual Gmail + Boomerang** (Starting out)
- Cost: Free to $15/month
- Setup: 5 minutes
- Best for: Lower volume, relationship-building
- Steps:
  1. Install Boomerang extension (boomeranggmail.com)
  2. Write personalized emails in Gmail
  3. Use Boomerang to schedule follow-ups
  4. Limit to 10-15 cold emails per day
  5. Hand-research each prospect (slower, but higher quality)

## WEEK 3: STRATEGY CALLS & CLOSING

### The 15-Minute Strategy Call Script

**Objective:** Learn their situation, show you understand their business, book the pilot.

**Duration:** 15 minutes (set a timer)

**Flow:**

**Opening (1 minute)**
```
"Thanks for hopping on! I know you're busy, so I'll keep this brief.

I spent some time reviewing your copy—emails, ads, product page—and I saw
some real opportunities to improve performance. But before I go into that,
I want to understand your business first.

Sound good?"
```

**Discovery (5 minutes)**
- "What's your biggest challenge right now with email or ads?"
- "How are you currently measuring success?" (listen for: "not sure" or vague metrics)
- "How much revenue do you think better copy could add?" (listen for number)
- "Who makes the decision on marketing changes?" (learn the buyer)

*Purpose: Understand their pain point + confirm they're a decision-maker*

**Demo (6 minutes)**
- "Based on what I saw, here are the 3 things I'd focus on:"
- Show 1-2 specific examples from their copy
- Explain what's wrong and what better looks like
- Use their numbers: "If your open rate goes from 22% to 28%, that's an extra 500 emails opened per campaign. At 3% CTR, that's 15 extra clicks. At 2.5% conversion, that's 1 extra customer per campaign. At your AOV, that's $X per month."

*Purpose: Show expertise + translate to revenue*

**Close (3 minutes)**
- "I think there's real opportunity here. Here's what I propose:"
- Pitch the pilot: "Let's do a 4-week pilot. I'll rewrite your welcome sequence and run 2 rounds of ad copy. We track everything. If it works, we keep going. If it doesn't, we don't continue. Sound fair?"
- State the price: "$3,000 for the month. That covers complete rewrite of your email sequence and 2 rounds of ad testing."
- Ask for the next step: "If you're interested, I can send over a 1-page agreement today. We'd kick off Monday. Does that work?"

**Handling Objections:**

*Objection: "That's expensive"*
Response: "I hear you. Here's how I think about it: If we add even one extra sale per month, that's paid for itself. Most of my clients add 3-5 extra sales from better email copy alone. But I get it—let me ask you this: If this worked and added $5K in extra revenue this month, would it feel expensive?"

*Objection: "I need to talk to my team"*
Response: "Totally makes sense. Here's what I'll do: I'll send you a 1-page summary of what we'd do, the timeline, and the investment. You can share it with your team. Then we can hop back on next week if there are questions. Does that work?"

*Objection: "We're not sure if we're ready for this"*
Response: "I understand. But here's the thing: You're already spending on ads and building your email list. The difference is whether that traffic converts at 2% or 4%. That gap is just copy. Why wait? Let's do a 4-week test. Worst case, you've spent $3K and learned something. Best case, you've got a blueprint for growth."

**If They Say Yes:**
- "Great! I'm excited. Here's what happens next: I'll send you an agreement and an onboarding form. The form asks questions about your business, audience, and goals. Once I get that back, we kick off Monday. Sound good?"
- Send agreement within 2 hours
- Get them to sign and pay within 24 hours

**If They Say No:**
- "No problem. Do you mind if I send you a few case studies in a couple weeks? Sometimes seeing results makes it easier to say yes next time."
- "Is there anything that would make this make more sense for you right now?"
- Leave the door open for follow-up in 2-3 weeks

## Objection Handling Playbook

### Common Objections & Responses

**"We already have a copywriter"**
Response: "Totally. How's it going? Are you seeing improvement in open rates / conversions? ...

If yes: 'Great! If your copywriter is crushing it, no need to change anything. But if you ever want a second opinion or need extra capacity, you know where to find me.'

If no: 'Yeah, a lot of brands do. The challenge is most copywriters are generalists. I specialize specifically in DTC email and ad copy. We bring a different lens. Would it hurt to get a second opinion?'"

**"Our cost structure doesn't support that investment right now"**
Response: "I get it. Here's how I think about it: You're probably spending $X on paid ads every month. Better copy means your ads convert better. One extra sale per month pays for this entire month. How many extra sales would you need for this to make sense? ...

[Listen to their number]

Based on what I've seen in your copy, I think we can hit that. Why not test it for one month?"

**"Can you send us some case studies / examples?"**
Response: "Of course. But honestly, the best way to evaluate this is to see it against your own business. Here's what I'll do: I'll create a quick rewrite of your welcome email. You compare it to what you have now. If you like the direction, we talk next week. If not, no hard feelings.

Can I get a copy of your current welcome sequence?"

**"We're happy with where things are"**
Response: "That's great. Most of my best clients say the same thing—right before they realize there's 30-50% more opportunity in their funnel.

Here's what I'm thinking: No commitment. Let me spend 20 minutes on a strategic audit. You see some specific ideas. If it looks interesting, we talk. If not, you've got a free analysis and some ideas to test internally.

Fair?"

**"We need to think about it"**
Response: "Totally makes sense. This isn't a decision you make on the spot. Here's what I'll do: I'll send you a 1-page summary of what we'd do, the results you could expect, and how the process works.

You can sit with your team on it. We'll hop back on next Tuesday if you want to move forward. Sound good?"

## Key Success Factors

### Do These Things Right:

1. **Personalization Over Volume**
   - Spend 5 min researching each prospect
   - Mention something specific from their website/ads
   - NOT: "Hi [FIRST_NAME], I help DTC brands..."
   - YES: "I noticed your welcome email doesn't mention social proof..."

2. **Make the Free Audit Valuable**
   - Spend time on it
   - Be specific (not vague)
   - Include an example of what better looks like
   - Make them feel like they learned something

3. **Follow Up**
   - Don't expect 50% response rate to cold emails
   - Expect 5-10%
   - Follow up is where the magic happens
   - 40% of deals close on follow-up #3+

4. **Filter for Right-Fit Clients**
   - Are they DTC? (Not B2B)
   - Do they have an email list? (At least 5K)
   - Do they run paid ads? (Budget > $1K/month)
   - Are they growing? (Want to invest in optimization)

5. **Close Fast**
   - If someone says "yes," get them to sign same day
   - Don't lose momentum
   - Payment before you start work
   - Start delivery on Monday if they close Friday

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Sending Too Many Emails Too Fast**
   - You'll burn out your domain
   - Prospect list is small enough to hand-research
   - Start with 10-15 per day, max

2. **Generic Subject Lines**
   - Subject: "Copywriting Service" (Bad)
   - Subject: "Your welcome email is missing social proof" (Good)
   - Specific > generic, always

3. **Trying to Close on First Email**
   - First email: Build interest
   - Second email: Share the audit
   - Third email: Offer the call
   - Don't push too hard, too fast

4. **Not Following Up**
   - 80% of deals close on follow-up
   - Don't give up after one "no thanks"
   - Persistence = professionalism (if done right)

5. **Undercharging**
   - $3K for a 4-week pilot is the minimum
   - You're adding $5-20K in value
   - Don't discount to close
   - Only discount if they commit to 3+ months

6. **Not Getting Access**
   - You can't audit what you can't see
   - Require email platform access
   - Require Facebook Ad account access
   - This also filters for serious prospects

7. **Over-Complicating the Scope**
   - Stick to: Email sequences + Meta ads
   - Option to add: Product page rewrite
   - Don't go into landing pages, funnels, retargeting
   - You'll get lost in scope creep
