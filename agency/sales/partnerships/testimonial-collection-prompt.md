# Testimonial Collection Prompt

> **Purpose:** Claude prompt that generates personalized testimonial request emails and follow-ups, plus a framework for turning raw client feedback into polished testimonials for marketing use.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad when requesting and processing client testimonials

## Contents

### Part 1: Testimonial Request Generator
- Input: client name, results achieved, relationship duration, their communication style
- Output: personalized email requesting a testimonial with specific prompting questions
- Questions designed to elicit specific, results-focused quotes (not generic "they're great!" feedback)
- Suggested questions:
  - "What was your content situation like before working with us?"
  - "What specific results have you seen?"
  - "What surprised you most about working with CopyDTC?"
  - "Would you recommend us to a friend? What would you tell them?"

### Part 2: Testimonial Polish Prompt
- Input: raw testimonial text from client
- Output: polished versions for different uses:
  - Full testimonial (2-3 paragraphs, for case studies and proposals)
  - Short quote (1-2 sentences, for website and social)
  - One-liner (under 15 words, for ad social proof)
  - Video testimonial script prompts (if they're willing to record)

### Part 3: Testimonial Placement Guide
- Where to use testimonials: website, proposals, outreach emails, social media, ad copy
- Matching testimonials to contexts (e.g., email results testimonial goes in email service proposals)
- Legal: always get written permission before using client quotes publicly
