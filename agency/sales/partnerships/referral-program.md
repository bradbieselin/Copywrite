# Referral Program

> **Purpose:** Structure and materials for CopyDTC's referral program. Incentivizes existing clients, partners, and network contacts to refer new business.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad when setting up and managing the referral program

## Contents

### Program Structure
- Referral reward: 10% of first 3 months' revenue from referred client (paid monthly)
- Alternative: $500 flat fee per closed referral (simpler, choose based on preference)
- Qualifying referral: must sign a contract and pay first invoice
- Payment timing: reward paid within 30 days of referred client's first payment

### Who Can Refer
- Current clients (best source — they experience the quality firsthand)
- Agency partners (web dev, design, paid media agencies)
- Ecommerce consultants and coaches
- Platform partners (Shopify experts, Klaviyo partners)

### Referral Process
1. Referrer introduces CopyDTC to prospect (email intro or shared contact)
2. Brad follows up within 24 hours
3. Standard sales process (audit → proposal → close)
4. Upon close, referrer is notified and reward is tracked
5. Reward paid per schedule

### Materials Needed
- Referral program one-pager (to share with potential referrers)
- Email template for requesting referrals from happy clients
- Thank you / reward notification emails
- Tracking spreadsheet or CRM integration

### Launch Plan
- Phase 1: Ask top 3 happiest clients for referrals (personal ask, no formal program needed)
- Phase 2: Formalize program with one-pager and tracking
- Phase 3: Expand to partner network
