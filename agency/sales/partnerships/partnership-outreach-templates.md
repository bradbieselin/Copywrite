# Partnership Outreach Templates

> **Purpose:** Email templates for reaching out to potential partners — agencies, consultants, and platforms that serve the same DTC brands we do but with complementary services.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad when building the partner network

## Contents

### Partner Types & Outreach Angles

#### 1. Shopify Development Agencies
- Angle: "Your clients need great copy for the sites you build"
- Value prop: we make their builds perform better with conversion-focused copy
- Template: collaborative, peer-to-peer tone

#### 2. Paid Media / Performance Marketing Agencies
- Angle: "Better ad copy = better ROAS for your clients"
- Value prop: we write the ads and landing pages that make their media spend work harder
- Template: results-focused, data-driven tone

#### 3. Email Marketing Agencies (Klaviyo Partners)
- Angle: "We write the emails, you manage the flows and strategy"
- Value prop: dedicated copy team that integrates with their email workflow
- Template: complementary services framing

#### 4. Ecommerce Consultants / Coaches
- Angle: "Your clients need execution, not just strategy"
- Value prop: we're the execution layer for their content recommendations
- Template: referral partnership framing

#### 5. Design Agencies
- Angle: "Great design needs great words"
- Value prop: we provide the copy that makes their designs convert
- Template: creative collaboration tone

### Per-Template Structure
- Subject line (3 options)
- Body (100-150 words)
- Specific value proposition for their business
- CTA (coffee chat or quick call)
- Follow-up email (7 days later)
