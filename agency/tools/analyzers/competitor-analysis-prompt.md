# Competitor Analysis Prompt

> **Purpose:** Claude prompt for generating a detailed competitor content analysis. Compares a client's content strategy and execution against 2-3 key competitors to identify gaps and opportunities.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad during client onboarding, quarterly strategy reviews, and prospect audits

## Contents

### The Prompt
- Input: client brand name and website
- Input: 2-3 competitor brand names and websites
- Input: channels to compare (email, website, social, ads, blog)
- Input: specific questions the client wants answered (optional)

### Output Sections
1. **Competitive Landscape Overview** — market positioning map of all brands analyzed
2. **Channel-by-Channel Comparison**
   - Website: messaging, structure, conversion elements, brand voice
   - Email: frequency, types, subject line style, design approach
   - Social media: platforms, posting frequency, content mix, engagement style
   - Ads: platforms, creative approach, messaging angles, offers
   - Blog/SEO: topics covered, posting frequency, keyword strategy
3. **Voice & Messaging Comparison** — how each brand talks to its audience
4. **Strength/Weakness Matrix** — where each brand excels and falls short
5. **White Space Opportunities** — content types or angles no competitor is doing well
6. **Actionable Recommendations** — specific ways the client can differentiate through content
7. **Quick Wins** — 3-5 things the client can do immediately based on competitive insights

### Quality Bar
- Analysis should reveal genuine insights, not just describe what each brand does
- Focus on actionable differences, not surface-level observations
- Identify opportunities that are both impactful and achievable
- Total output: 3-5 pages
