### 9. Onboarding Questionnaire Delivery

**When to send:** Within 24 hours of welcome email
**Subject:** Your brand questionnaire — the foundation of everything we'll build

Hey [FIRST_NAME],

Attached is your brand questionnaire. This is the single most important thing you'll do in our onboarding — it's how we learn to sound exactly like [BRAND_NAME].

A few things to know:

- **Time:** About 30-45 minutes. Block it off, don't try to rush it between meetings.
- **Honesty > polish:** Write how you actually talk about your brand, not how you think you should. Raw and real is more useful than polished and vague.
- **No wrong answers.** If a question doesn't apply, skip it. If you want to ramble on one, go for it. More is always better here.

I need it back by **[DATE — 48 hours from send]** so we can stay on schedule for your Brand Voice Bible.

If you'd rather talk through it instead of typing, let me know — we can do a 30-minute call and I'll capture everything.

Brad

## Usage Notes

- **Always personalize.** A template is a starting point. If you can reference something specific about the client or their brand, do it.
- **Subject lines matter.** Test variations. The subject lines above are defaults — adjust based on what you know about the recipient.
- **Timing matters.** Send prospect emails Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM in the recipient's timezone. Client emails can go anytime during business hours.
- **Don't stack follow-ups.** If someone hasn't responded to two emails, wait at least a week before the third touch. After three with no response, move on.
- **BCC yourself** on every client email so you have a record. Or use a CRM that tracks this automatically.
