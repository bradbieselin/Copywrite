# Monthly Business Review Prompt

> **Purpose:** Claude prompt that generates a comprehensive monthly business review for CopyDTC. Input your numbers, get back a strategic analysis with actionable insights.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad at the end of each month for strategic planning

## Contents

### The Prompt
- Input: revenue tracker data for the month
- Input: client health statuses
- Input: sales pipeline data
- Input: key metrics from KPI definitions
- Input: goals from last month's review

### Output Sections
1. **Revenue Analysis** — MRR trend, revenue by client, revenue by tier, month-over-month growth
2. **Client Portfolio Health** — retention rate, satisfaction scores, at-risk clients, upsell opportunities
3. **Sales Performance** — pipeline analysis, conversion rates, outreach effectiveness, CAC
4. **Operational Efficiency** — capacity utilization, turnaround times, quality metrics
5. **Content Performance** — aggregate client results (email open rates, ad ROAS, etc.)
6. **Strategic Insights** — patterns, opportunities, and threats identified from the data
7. **Next Month Goals** — 3-5 specific, measurable goals with owner and timeline
8. **Quarterly Trajectory** — are we on track for quarterly goals? What needs to change?

### Quality Bar
- Analysis should be honest, not optimistic
- Every insight should come with a specific action
- Compare actuals to targets from KPI definitions
- Total output: 1-2 pages of actionable analysis
