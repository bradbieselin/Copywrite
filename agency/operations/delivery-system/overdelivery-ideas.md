# Overdelivery Ideas

> **Purpose:** A running list of ways to surprise clients with unexpected value, building loyalty and increasing retention. These are small, high-impact gestures that cost us minimal time but create outsized goodwill.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad (and future account managers) throughout the client relationship, especially during the pilot month

## Contents

### Quick Wins (Under 30 Minutes)
- Unsolicited subject line alternatives for upcoming campaigns
- Competitor content teardown with actionable insights
- Holiday/event-specific content ideas relevant to their brand
- Quick SEO audit of their top product page
- Social media caption refresh for their worst-performing recent post

### Medium Effort (30-60 Minutes)
- Bonus email template for an upcoming holiday or event
- Content repurposing suggestions (turn blog → social → email)
- Audience persona deep-dive based on their analytics
- Competitive positioning analysis
- Monthly content trends briefing relevant to their industry

### High Impact (1-2 Hours)
- Full content audit of a section of their site
- Strategic content roadmap for the next quarter
- Brand voice evolution report (how their voice should mature as they grow)
- Customer journey content map (what content is missing at each stage)

### When to Deploy
- During pilot month (overdelivery is critical for retention)
- After a difficult feedback round (rebuild confidence)
- Before the retention conversation at month-end
- When a client has been quiet (re-engage them)
- On client anniversaries or business milestones

### Rules
- Never overdeliver so much that it sets unsustainable expectations
- Always frame overdelivery as "we noticed this and thought it could help" — not "look how much extra work we did"
- Track what overdelivery resonated most with each client for future reference
