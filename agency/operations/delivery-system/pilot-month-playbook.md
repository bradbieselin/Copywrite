# Pilot Month Playbook

## Purpose
Day-by-day guide for the first 30 days with a new client. The pilot month is your most important retention tool. You overdeliver, build trust, and make it obvious that continuing is worth it. This playbook tells you exactly what to do, when, and how.

**Used by:** Brad (and eventually contractors) for every new client's first 30 days.

**Goal:** By Day 30, the client has 25+ deliverables in hand, measurable early results, and zero desire to leave.

---

## The Pilot Month Promise

Every pilot month client gets:
- 25 deliverables (5 more than the Starter tier's standard 20)
- A bonus content strategy document (90-day roadmap for their brand)
- 48-hour turnaround on all deliverables
- A dedicated Slack channel or email thread for direct communication
- End-of-month results review with upgrade recommendation

**Your internal goal:** Make this client so happy in 30 days that they feel like they're underpaying at $3,000/month.

---

## Pre-Pilot Setup (Days -3 to 0)

Before the client's official start date, get these things done:

### Day -3: Internal Prep
- [ ] Create client folder using `operations/onboarding/client-folder-template/`
- [ ] Set up their Slack channel or dedicated email thread
- [ ] Review their website, emails, and social media (20-minute scan)
- [ ] Note 3 quick wins you can deliver in Week 1
- [ ] Prepare the client questionnaire for sending (`operations/onboarding/client-questionnaire.md`)

### Day -1: Send the Questionnaire
- [ ] Send the client questionnaire with a Loom video (2-3 minutes) explaining what it is, why it matters, and how long it takes (30-45 minutes)
- [ ] Set a 48-hour deadline for completion: "The sooner we get this back, the sooner we start delivering."
- [ ] Include your direct contact for questions

**Loom script outline:**
> "Hey [NAME], excited to get started. I'm sending over our Brand Questionnaire — this is how we make sure everything we create sounds like [BRAND], not like a template. It takes about 30-45 minutes and covers your brand voice, audience, competitors, and goals. The faster we get this back, the faster we start shipping content. If any question doesn't apply or you're not sure, just skip it and we'll cover it in our kickoff call."

## Week 1: Foundation (Days 1-7)

**Theme:** Build the brand foundation and deliver 3-5 quick wins.

### Day 1: Kickoff Call
- [ ] 30-minute video call with the client
- [ ] Review questionnaire answers together (fill in any gaps live)
- [ ] Confirm priority deliverables: "What do you need most in the next 7 days?"
- [ ] Agree on communication cadence (daily Slack updates? weekly email?)
- [ ] Set expectations: "You'll see your first deliverables within 48 hours."

**Kickoff call agenda (30 minutes):**

| Time | Topic | Goal |
|------|-------|------|
| 0-5 min | Intro and rapport | Build the relationship |
| 5-15 min | Questionnaire review | Fill gaps, clarify voice |
| 15-22 min | Priority alignment | Confirm what to build first |
| 22-27 min | Process and communication | Set expectations clearly |
| 27-30 min | Next steps | Confirm Day 3 delivery |

### Day 2: Build the Brand Voice Bible
- [ ] Run the completed questionnaire through `operations/onboarding/brand-voice-generator-prompt.md`
- [ ] Review the generated Bible for accuracy and completeness
- [ ] Send the Bible to the client: "Here's your Brand Voice Bible. Take a look and let me know if anything feels off. This is the foundation for everything we create."
- [ ] Ask for feedback: "Does this capture your brand voice? Anything you'd add or change?"

### Day 3: First Deliverables
- [ ] Deliver 3-5 pieces based on the client's stated priorities
- [ ] Use the Brand Voice Bible for all content
- [ ] Run every piece through `operations/delivery-system/quality-checklist.md`
- [ ] Send with a short note: "First batch is here. Let me know what you think — I want to dial in the voice before we ramp up."

**Recommended first deliverables by client type:**

| Client Need | First Batch (3-5 pieces) | Why These First |
|------------|-------------------------|----------------|
| "We need email help" | Welcome flow (3 emails) + 2 abandoned cart emails | Highest ROI, results measurable within days |
| "We need better ads" | 3 Meta ad sets + 2 Google search ad sets | Can launch immediately, quick feedback loop |
| "Our product pages need work" | 5 product descriptions | Visible improvement on-site, easy to compare before/after |
| "We need everything" | Welcome email (1) + 2 product descriptions + 2 social posts | Shows range, lets you assess what they react to most |

### Days 4-7: Iterate and Expand
- [ ] Process any Day 3 feedback immediately (same-day turnaround on revisions)
- [ ] Deliver 3-5 more pieces based on feedback and priorities
- [ ] Send a quick end-of-week check-in: "Week 1 recap: here's what we delivered, here's what's coming next week. Any adjustments?"

**Week 1 target:** 6-10 deliverables shipped. Client has given positive feedback on voice and quality.

---

## Week 2: Ramp Up (Days 8-14)

**Theme:** Hit full delivery speed and establish the rhythm.

### Days 8-10: Full Production
- [ ] Deliver 6-8 pieces across multiple content types
- [ ] Introduce at least one content type the client didn't specifically ask for (this is the overdelivery — a bonus blog post, an extra email, a social content set they didn't expect)
- [ ] Send each batch with a brief note explaining your thinking: "I included a post-purchase email sequence because your current flow ends after the confirmation email. This alone could increase repeat purchase rate by 15-20%."

### Day 10: Midpoint Check-In
- [ ] Send a Loom video (3-5 minutes) or have a quick call
- [ ] Recap what's been delivered so far (include a count)
- [ ] Share any early data or observations: "Your welcome email has been live for 5 days — here's what we're seeing..."
- [ ] Ask: "Is there anything we should prioritize for the next 2 weeks? Anything you want to change about the process?"
- [ ] Mention the bonus strategy document: "I'm also putting together a 90-day content roadmap for [BRAND] — you'll have that by end of month."

**Midpoint check-in template:**
> "Hey [NAME], quick update — we're at the halfway point of our pilot month. So far we've delivered [NUMBER] pieces across [CONTENT TYPES]. Here's what I'm seeing: [1-2 EARLY OBSERVATIONS]. For the next two weeks, I'm planning to focus on [UPCOMING PRIORITIES]. Does that match what you need? Also, I'm working on a 90-day content strategy doc for [BRAND] that I'll have for you by end of month. Let me know if you want to jump on a quick call to discuss."

### Days 11-14: Keep Shipping
- [ ] Deliver 4-6 more pieces
- [ ] Start tracking any available performance data (email open rates, ad CTR, etc.)
- [ ] Total by end of Week 2: 15-18 deliverables

---

## Week 3: Prove Value (Days 15-21)

**Theme:** Deliver the remaining pieces, start building the case for continuing.

### Days 15-18: Complete the Core Deliverables
- [ ] Deliver remaining pieces to hit 22-25 total
- [ ] Process any outstanding revisions (should be minimal by now — voice should be dialed in)
- [ ] Begin drafting the 90-day content strategy document

### Days 19-21: Strategy Document + Early Results
- [ ] Complete the 90-day content strategy document. Include:
  - What we delivered in the pilot month (full list)
  - Early performance data (if available)
  - What we'd do in months 2-3 at the current tier
  - What we'd do at the Growth tier (this is your upsell setup)
  - Content calendar for the next 30 days
- [ ] Compile all available results data for the end-of-month review

**90-day strategy document outline:**

| Section | What to Include |
|---------|----------------|
| Pilot Month Summary | List every deliverable, organized by type. Include word counts and any early metrics. |
| What's Working | 2-3 things that performed well, with data if available. |
| What to Double Down On | The content types or channels showing the most promise. |
| Month 2 Plan (Current Tier) | What we'd deliver if they stay at Starter. |
| Month 2-3 Plan (Growth Tier) | What we'd deliver if they upgrade. Frame this as "what more looks like." |
| 90-Day Content Calendar | High-level calendar showing planned content across all channels. |
| Recommended Next Steps | Your clear recommendation for which tier makes sense going forward. |

---

## Week 4: Close the Loop (Days 22-30)

**Theme:** Deliver final pieces, present results, convert to ongoing engagement.

### Days 22-25: Final Deliverables + Polish
- [ ] Deliver any remaining pieces (target: 25 total by Day 25)
- [ ] Ensure all revisions are complete and final files are in the client's folder
- [ ] Finalize the 90-day strategy document
- [ ] Prepare the end-of-month results review

### Day 26-27: End-of-Month Review
- [ ] Schedule a 30-minute call
- [ ] Walk through the results review:

**End-of-month review agenda (30 minutes):**

| Time | Topic | What to Cover |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 0-3 min | Recap | "Here's what we set out to do 30 days ago." |
| 3-10 min | Deliverables review | Show the full list: 25 pieces across X content types. |
| 10-18 min | Results | Share any data: open rates, click rates, conversion improvements, ad performance. Even if data is limited, frame early indicators positively. |
| 18-23 min | Strategy doc walkthrough | Present the 90-day roadmap. Emphasize what's possible at Growth tier. |
| 23-28 min | Recommendation | "Based on what we've seen, I'd recommend [TIER] going forward. Here's why." |
| 28-30 min | Next steps | If they say yes, confirm the tier and schedule Month 2 kickoff. If they need time, give them 3-5 days and follow up. |

### Day 28-30: Conversion or Follow-Up
- [ ] If they committed on the call: send the service agreement, set up billing, and begin Month 2 planning
- [ ] If they need time: send a follow-up email within 24 hours with the strategy doc attached and a clear next step

**Follow-up email template (for undecided clients):**
> "Hey [NAME], thanks for the call today. I've attached the 90-day content strategy we discussed. Here's the quick version: in the pilot month, we delivered [NUMBER] pieces and saw [KEY RESULT]. At the Growth tier, we'd be doing [WHAT GROWTH LOOKS LIKE]. I think [BRAND] has real upside here and I'd love to keep building on what we started. Let me know what makes sense for you — happy to answer any questions."

## Overdelivery Moments

These are specific actions designed to make the client think "I can't believe they did that." Build at least 3 of these into every pilot month.

| Moment | When | What It Is | Why It Works |
|--------|------|-----------|-------------|
| The Surprise Deliverable | Week 2 | Include a content type they didn't ask for (bonus email, extra ad set, social content) | Shows initiative and generosity |
| The Proactive Fix | Anytime | Spot something on their site or in their emails that's broken and flag it (even if it's outside your scope) | Shows you're paying attention to their business, not just completing tasks |
| The Data Insight | Week 3 | Share an observation about their content performance with a recommendation | Positions you as a strategist, not just a writer |
| The Fast Turnaround | Anytime | Deliver something in 24 hours when they expected 48 | Builds trust that you're reliable and fast |
| The Strategy Doc | Week 3-4 | The 90-day content roadmap (this is the biggest overdelivery) | Gives them a vision of what's possible and makes the upgrade conversation easy |

## Red Flags to Watch For

If you see any of these during the pilot month, address them immediately. Don't wait for the end-of-month review.

| Red Flag | What It Means | What to Do |
|----------|--------------|-----------|
| Client hasn't given feedback by Day 7 | They're busy, disengaged, or unhappy but not saying so | Send a direct message: "I want to make sure we're on the right track. Can you take 5 minutes to review what we've sent so far?" |
| Revision requests change the voice significantly | The Brand Voice Bible might be off, or the client's vision doesn't match what they wrote | Schedule a 15-minute call to recalibrate. Update the Bible based on the conversation. |
| Client keeps adding to the scope | They're testing your boundaries or they don't understand what's included | Acknowledge the request, explain what's included in the pilot, and offer to add it as a paid add-on or include it at a higher tier. |
| Radio silence after Day 14 | Client might be losing interest or having buyer's remorse | Proactive outreach: send a Loom showing what you've delivered and what's coming. Make them feel the value. |
| Client compares you to their previous writer/agency | They're evaluating whether you're worth the switch | Don't get defensive. Ask: "What worked well with them and what didn't? I want to make sure we're doing more of the good stuff." |
| Payment issues | Late payment on the pilot month invoice | Address immediately and directly. Don't deliver more content until payment is resolved. |

## Pilot Month Metrics

Track these for every pilot client:

| Metric | Target | How to Track |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Deliverables completed | 25+ | Client folder in Google Drive/Notion |
| Time from questionnaire to first delivery | Under 48 hours | Internal timestamp |
| Average revision rounds per deliverable | Under 1.5 | Track in project management tool |
| Client response time (feedback turnaround) | Under 24 hours | Monitor Slack/email response times |
| Client NPS / satisfaction | 9+ out of 10 | Ask directly at end-of-month review |
| Conversion to ongoing engagement | 80%+ | Track in revenue tracker |
| Upgrade to Growth tier within 90 days | 50%+ | Track in revenue tracker |
| Total hours spent per pilot client | 20-25 hours | Time tracking tool (Toggl, Clockify) |

- [ ] Remaining core deliverables shipped (Days 15-18)
- [ ] 90-day strategy document drafted (Days 19-21)

- [ ] 22-25 total deliverables by end of Week 3

- [ ] All 25+ deliverables complete and final (Day 25)
