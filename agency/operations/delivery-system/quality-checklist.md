# Quality Checklist

> **Purpose:** Pre-delivery checklist that every piece of content must pass before being sent to a client. Ensures consistency, quality, and brand alignment across all deliverables.
> **Used by:** Brad (and future contractors) before delivering any content to a client
> **Rule:** No deliverable goes to a client until it passes all applicable checks below.

---

## Universal Checks (Apply to EVERY Deliverable)

### Voice & Brand Alignment

- [ ] **Does this sound like the client's brand?** Read it out loud. Would the founder say "yep, that's us"?
- [ ] **Check against the Brand Voice Bible:** are all "always use" words present where appropriate?
- [ ] **Check against the Brand Voice Bible:** are any "never use" words present? (Ctrl+F each one)
- [ ] **Is the tone calibrated correctly for this channel?** Email tone is different from ad tone is different from blog tone — check the Tone Spectrum section of the Voice Bible.
- [ ] **Does the writing level match the brand?** A luxury brand shouldn't sound casual. A fun snack brand shouldn't sound corporate.

### Copy Quality

- [ ] **Is the headline/hook the strongest possible version?** Write 3 alternatives. Is the current one still the best?
- [ ] **Are paragraphs 3 sentences or fewer?** Long paragraphs kill readability on screens.
- [ ] **Is every sentence doing work?** Delete any sentence and ask: does the piece get worse? If not, cut it.
- [ ] **Are benefits leading over features?** Features tell. Benefits sell. "Made with organic oats" → "Keeps you full until lunch without the crash."
- [ ] **Is there a clear CTA?** Does the reader know exactly what to do next?
- [ ] **Is the opening line strong enough to earn the second line?**
- [ ] **Read the last paragraph.** Does it drive action or just trail off?

### Technical & Accuracy

- [ ] **Zero spelling errors.** Run through a spell checker AND read manually — spell checkers miss context errors.
- [ ] **Zero grammar errors.** Intentional grammar breaks for voice are OK if the brand uses them.
- [ ] **All product names spelled correctly and capitalized consistently.**
- [ ] **All prices, percentages, and numbers are accurate.**
- [ ] **All claims are truthful and substantiated.** No "clinically proven" without a citation, no "#1 brand" without data.
- [ ] **Regulatory compliance check.** If the product is in a regulated category (supplements, skincare, health), are all claims FDA/FTC compliant? When in doubt, soften the claim or flag it for the client's legal review.
- [ ] **No placeholder text remaining.** All [BRAND_NAME], [PRODUCT], etc. have been replaced with real content.
- [ ] **No competitor names used negatively or incorrectly.**

### Formatting & Presentation

- [ ] **Clean formatting with consistent spacing.** No double spaces, no inconsistent line breaks.
- [ ] **Headers and subheads are used correctly** — not just bolded body text.
- [ ] **CTA buttons or links are clearly marked** with the exact text to use.
- [ ] **If there are images referenced, are the image directions clear enough for a designer to follow?**
- [ ] **Is the content the correct length per the brief?** Check word count against the template spec.

## Channel-Specific Checks

### Email Content

- [ ] **Subject line is under 50 characters** (ideally under 40 for mobile).
- [ ] **Preview text is written and under 90 characters.**
- [ ] **Subject line and preview text work together** — don't repeat each other.
- [ ] **Email body is scannable on mobile** — short lines, clear CTA button.
- [ ] **Dynamic tokens are correct:** [FIRST_NAME], [PRODUCT_NAME], etc. — no broken or mismatched tokens.
- [ ] **Unsubscribe/compliance footer is noted** (even if the client adds it — remind them).
- [ ] **Is there exactly ONE primary CTA?** Multiple CTAs kill click rates.
- [ ] **If this is part of a sequence, does it flow from the previous email and set up the next?**

### Landing Page Copy

- [ ] **Hero headline is under 10 words** and communicates the core value.
- [ ] **The page answers "what is this, who is it for, and why should I care" above the fold.**
- [ ] **Social proof is present** — testimonials, reviews, trust badges.
- [ ] **Objections are addressed before the final CTA.**
- [ ] **Page has a clear visual hierarchy** — heading sizes descend logically.
- [ ] **FAQ section answers real customer questions** — not just marketing questions in disguise.
- [ ] **Mobile layout is considered** — is the CTA visible without scrolling on a phone?

### Blog/SEO Content

- [ ] **Primary keyword appears in:** title, H1, first 100 words, 1–2 H2s, meta description.
- [ ] **Primary keyword density is 0.5–1.5%** — not stuffed, not absent.
- [ ] **Secondary keywords each appear 1–2 times naturally.**
- [ ] **Meta title is 50–60 characters** with keyword near the front.
- [ ] **Meta description is 150–160 characters** with keyword and a CTA.
- [ ] **All H2/H3 headers are informative** — not cute-but-vague.
- [ ] **Internal link placeholders are included** with suggested anchor text.
- [ ] **Content is genuinely useful.** Would this rank based on quality alone, ignoring SEO?

### Ad Copy (Meta / Google / TikTok)

- [ ] **Headlines are within platform character limits.** Meta: 40 chars. Google: 30 chars.
- [ ] **Descriptions are within platform character limits.** Meta: varies by placement. Google: 90 chars.
- [ ] **Hook/first line would stop a scroll.** Be honest — would YOU stop scrolling?
- [ ] **Creative direction notes are specific enough** for a designer or video editor.
- [ ] **CTA matches the campaign objective.** Don't say "Learn More" on a conversion campaign.
- [ ] **No prohibited claims for the ad platform.** Check Meta/Google ad policies for the product category.
- [ ] **Multiple variations provided for testing** — minimum 3 hooks, 2 body versions.

### Product Descriptions

- [ ] **Short description is 50–75 words** (for product grid/card view).
- [ ] **Long description is 150–300 words** (for product detail page).
- [ ] **Bullets start with benefits or outcomes, not features.**
- [ ] **Sensory language is used where appropriate** — how does it feel, taste, look, smell?
- [ ] **Primary objection is addressed.**
- [ ] **SEO meta description is written** (150–160 characters).

### Social Media Posts

- [ ] **Instagram captions have a strong hook before the "...more" truncation** — first 125 characters matter most.
- [ ] **TikTok scripts have a hook in the first 1–3 seconds.**
- [ ] **Twitter posts are under 280 characters** (ideally under 240 for better engagement).
- [ ] **Hashtags are relevant and properly formatted.** Instagram: 15–20. Twitter: 0–2. TikTok: 3–5.
- [ ] **Visual direction is clear and specific.**
- [ ] **Content matches the platform's culture.** TikTok ≠ LinkedIn.

### SMS Messages

- [ ] **Under 160 characters** (including link placeholder and opt-out).
- [ ] **One emoji maximum.**
- [ ] **Clear CTA with link placeholder.**
- [ ] **Opt-out language included:** "Reply STOP to unsubscribe."
- [ ] **Reads like a text from a friend, not a marketing blast.**

---

## Final Gate

Before hitting send on ANY deliverable to ANY client:

- [ ] **Would I put this in my portfolio?** If no, it's not ready.
- [ ] **If I were the client and I paid $3,000–$10,000/month for this, would I be happy?** If no, revise.
- [ ] **Does this piece SELL or does it just EXIST?** Every piece of content should drive toward a business outcome. If it's just filling a content calendar spot, rewrite with purpose.

---

## How to Use This Checklist

1. **Writer completes the deliverable.**
2. **Writer runs through the Universal Checks + relevant Channel-Specific Checks.**
3. **Writer fixes any failures.** No deliverable should reach review with known issues.
4. **If this is contractor work:** Brad reviews against the same checklist.
5. **Only after passing all applicable checks** does the content go to the client.
6. **Log any recurring issues** in the Brand Voice Bible under "Common Pitfalls" — build this section over time for each client.

### Estimated Review Times

| Deliverable Type | Review Time |
|---|---|
| Email / Social post | 5–10 minutes |
| Ad copy / Product description | 3–5 minutes |
| Landing page / Blog post | 10–15 minutes |
