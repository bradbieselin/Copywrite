# Client Communication SOP

> **Purpose:** Standard operating procedure for all client communications — how we write emails, handle feedback, manage expectations, and maintain professional relationships.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** All CopyDTC team members who interact with clients

## Contents

### Communication Principles
- Respond within 4 business hours (even if just to acknowledge receipt)
- Be proactive — share updates before clients ask for them
- Be specific — "I'll have this to you by Thursday at 3pm" not "I'll get to this soon"
- Be honest — if something is delayed, say so immediately with a new timeline
- Match the client's communication style (formal clients get formal emails, casual clients get casual ones)

### Email Templates Reference
- See email-templates.md for all standard client email templates
- Always personalize templates — never send a template that reads like a template

### Response Time Standards
- General inquiries: within 4 business hours
- Feedback on deliverables: acknowledged within 2 hours, revisions within 24 hours
- Urgent requests: within 1 hour during business hours
- After-hours: acknowledge next morning by 9am

### Feedback Handling
- Thank the client for feedback — always, even if it's critical
- Summarize what you heard back to them to confirm understanding
- Provide a specific timeline for revisions
- If feedback requires significant changes, schedule a call instead of email ping-pong

### Difficult Conversations
- Scope creep: "That's a great idea. It falls outside our current scope, but here's how we can add it..."
- Missed deadlines: own it immediately, provide new timeline, explain what you're doing to prevent recurrence
- Unhappy client: listen first, acknowledge their frustration, present a concrete plan to fix it
- Price increases: lead with the value they've received, explain the reason, give advance notice

### Meeting Standards
- Every meeting has an agenda sent 24 hours in advance
- Every meeting ends with clear action items and owners
- Recap email sent within 2 hours of meeting end
- Keep meetings to 30 or 45 minutes — respect their time

### Tools
- Email: primary communication channel
- Slack: for clients who prefer real-time chat (set boundaries)
- Calendly: for scheduling calls
- Loom: for async video updates when a call isn't necessary
