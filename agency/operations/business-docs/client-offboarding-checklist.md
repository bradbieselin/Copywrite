# Client Offboarding Checklist

> **Purpose:** Ensure a clean, professional offboarding when a client relationship ends. Protect the relationship for future referrals, collect a testimonial if appropriate, and tie up all loose ends.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad when a client decides not to continue

## Contents

### Immediate Actions
- [ ] Acknowledge the client's decision professionally (no guilt trips)
- [ ] Confirm the last day of service and final deliverables
- [ ] Complete any in-progress work
- [ ] Deliver all outstanding content

### Final Deliverables
- [ ] All remaining content from the current month's calendar
- [ ] Updated Brand Voice Bible (client keeps this)
- [ ] Content performance summary for the engagement period
- [ ] Recommendations for maintaining content quality going forward

### Administrative
- [ ] Final invoice sent (prorate if mid-month)
- [ ] Revoke access to internal tools and systems
- [ ] Archive client folder (move from active to archive)
- [ ] Update CRM/project management status

### Relationship Preservation
- [ ] Send a personal thank-you email
- [ ] If results were good: request a testimonial
- [ ] If relationship was strong: ask for referrals
- [ ] Offer a "door is always open" message with a specific re-engagement incentive
- [ ] Add to quarterly check-in list (reach out in 90 days)

### Internal Review
- [ ] Document what went well and what didn't (internal only)
- [ ] Identify any process improvements based on this engagement
- [ ] If client churned due to quality: review and update templates/processes
- [ ] If client churned due to price: review pricing strategy
- [ ] Update churn metrics in dashboard
