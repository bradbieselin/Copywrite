# Service Agreement Template

> **Purpose:** Master service agreement between CopyDTC and clients. Covers scope, pricing, terms, intellectual property, confidentiality, and termination. To be reviewed by a lawyer before use.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad when signing new clients

## Contents

### Agreement Structure
1. **Parties** — CopyDTC (Provider) and Client details
2. **Services** — Description of services by tier (Starter/Growth/Scale)
3. **Scope of Work** — Specific deliverables, quantities, and timelines
4. **Pricing & Payment** — Monthly retainer amount, payment terms (net 15), late payment policy
5. **Term & Renewal** — Initial pilot month, then month-to-month with 30-day notice
6. **Intellectual Property** — All deliverables become client property upon full payment
7. **Confidentiality** — Mutual NDA covering brand info, strategies, and business data
8. **Revisions** — Included revision rounds per deliverable, process for additional revisions
9. **Client Responsibilities** — Timely feedback, access to assets, questionnaire completion
10. **Limitation of Liability** — Standard limitation clause
11. **Termination** — 30-day written notice, final deliverables, offboarding process
12. **Portfolio Rights** — CopyDTC may use anonymized samples in portfolio unless client opts out
13. **Governing Law** — Jurisdiction and dispute resolution
14. **Signatures** — Both parties

### Key Terms to Customize Per Client
- Tier and monthly rate
- Specific deliverables included
- Any custom terms or exclusions
- Start date and pilot month end date
