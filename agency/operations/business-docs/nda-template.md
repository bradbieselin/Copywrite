# NDA Template

> **Purpose:** Mutual non-disclosure agreement template for use with clients, contractors, and partners. Protects both parties' confidential information.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad when engaging with new clients or contractors who require a standalone NDA (note: the service agreement includes confidentiality terms, so this is for situations where a separate NDA is needed)

## Contents

### Agreement Structure
1. **Parties** — CopyDTC and the other party
2. **Definition of Confidential Information** — broad but clear definition covering:
   - Business strategies, pricing, and client lists
   - Brand voice documents, content strategies, and proprietary prompts
   - Financial information and business metrics
   - Any information marked as confidential
3. **Exclusions** — public information, independently developed info, legally required disclosures
4. **Obligations** — protect with reasonable care, limit access to need-to-know, don't disclose without written consent
5. **Term** — 2 years from date of disclosure (or duration of engagement + 1 year)
6. **Return of Materials** — return or destroy confidential materials upon request or termination
7. **Remedies** — injunctive relief available for breach
8. **Governing Law** — jurisdiction
9. **Signatures** — both parties with date

### When to Use This
- Client requests a standalone NDA before sharing sensitive brand information
- Contractor onboarding (if not covered by contractor agreement)
- Partnership discussions with other agencies or platforms
- Due diligence for larger enterprise clients
