# Report Email Template

> **Purpose:** Email template for sending monthly performance reports to clients. The email should make the client excited to open the report and feel confident in our partnership.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad when delivering monthly reports to clients

## Contents

### Email Structure
- Subject line: "[BRAND_NAME] × CopyDTC — [Month] Performance Report"
- Opening: 2-3 sentences highlighting the biggest win of the month
- Key metrics snapshot: 3-4 headline numbers inline (no need to open the report to get the gist)
- Report link/attachment note
- Preview of next month: 1-2 sentences teasing what's coming
- CTA: schedule the review call if not already booked
- Sign-off

### Tone Guidelines
- Confident but not cocky
- Data-driven but human
- Forward-looking — always end on momentum
- Short — this email should take 30 seconds to read

### Variants
- Variant A: Strong month (lead with the biggest metric win)
- Variant B: Average month (lead with a strategic insight)
- Variant C: Tough month (lead with what we learned and what we're changing)
