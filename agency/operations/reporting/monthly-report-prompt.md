# Monthly Report Generator Prompt

> **Purpose:** Claude prompt that generates a professional monthly performance report for a client, summarizing deliverables, metrics, insights, and the plan for next month.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad at the end of each month for every active client

## Contents

### The Prompt
- Input: list of deliverables produced this month (from content calendar)
- Input: available performance metrics (email open rates, click rates, landing page conversions, ad performance, etc.)
- Input: client goals from onboarding questionnaire
- Input: any notable wins, feedback, or challenges from the month

### Output Sections
1. **Executive Summary** — 3-4 sentences on what was accomplished and the key takeaway
2. **Deliverables Summary** — table of everything produced, organized by type, with status
3. **Performance Metrics** — key numbers with month-over-month comparison where available
4. **Wins & Highlights** — what worked best and why
5. **Insights & Recommendations** — what the data tells us and what to do about it
6. **Challenges & Adjustments** — what didn't work and how we're adapting
7. **Next Month Plan** — preview of Month 2 content calendar with strategic rationale
8. **Action Items** — clear next steps for both CopyDTC and the client

### Formatting
- Clean, professional layout suitable for PDF export
- Charts/graphs suggested where data supports them
- Executive summary should stand alone (client may only read this)
- Total length: 2-3 pages
