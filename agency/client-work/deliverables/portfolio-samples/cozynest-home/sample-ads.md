## Creative Direction Notes

**For Meta ads:**
- **Static images:** Lifestyle photography in real bedrooms, not studio sets. Show rumpled, lived-in beds with natural light — the "effortless" look is central to the brand. Always show texture close-ups (the linen weave is the hero). Avoid anything that looks like a hotel or catalog shoot.
- **Video (15s):** Slow-motion hand running across linen fabric, followed by a quick stat card ("Gets softer every wash. Lasts 10–15 years. $148."). Keep it sensory, quiet, and unhurried. No voiceover needed — text overlays and music.
- **Carousel:** Color comparison carousel — each card shows one color (Fog, Oat, Sand, Cloud, Olive, Terracotta) styled in a different bedroom. Let people swipe through to find their match.

**For Pinterest:**
- Vertical images (2:3 ratio). Text overlay should be minimal and clean.
- Bedroom styling should feel "real home" — not a magazine spread. Include small imperfections (a book, a wrinkled corner, a pet on the bed) to signal authenticity.
- Educational pins (cotton vs. linen, care guides, color matching tips) drive saves and long-term traffic.
