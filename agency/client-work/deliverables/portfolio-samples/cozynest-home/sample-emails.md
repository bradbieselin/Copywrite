### Email 4: The Care Guide (Day 6)

**Subject:** The washing mistake that ruins most nice sheets
**Preview text:** It's not the temperature. It's not the detergent.

Hey [FIRST_NAME],

The #1 thing that ruins good sheets isn't the washing machine. It's the dryer.

Here's the deal:

**Washing:** Machine wash on cold or warm. Use a gentle, unscented detergent (we like Branch Basics or Seventh Generation Free & Clear). That's it. Linen doesn't need special treatment.

**Drying — this is where it matters:**
- **Best:** Line dry or hang over a door/rack. Takes 2–3 hours.
- **Good:** Tumble dry on LOW heat for 10–15 minutes to soften, then air dry the rest.
- **What ruins them:** High heat in the dryer. Linen fibers are strong but high heat breaks them down over time. It's the difference between sheets that last 5 years and sheets that last 15.

**Ironing:** Don't. Linen is supposed to look relaxed and lived-in. The natural wrinkles are the point. If you must smooth them out, a quick tumble in the dryer on no-heat does the trick.

**Pro tip:** Wash your linen sheets before first use. They're already stone-washed, but that first home wash kicks off the softening process and removes any residual sizing from the weaving process. You'll notice the difference immediately.
