## Brand Voice Summary

CozyNest sounds like your most tasteful friend who just renovated her apartment — someone who knows the difference between cheap linen and real linen and can explain why it matters without sounding like a snob. The brand is warm, specific, and sensory — every piece of copy should make you feel the weight of the fabric and see the light falling on it. While competitors in the home textiles space either go full luxury-aspirational (marble countertops and champagne flutes) or full earthy-crunchy (hemp everything and composting tutorials), CozyNest occupies the realistic middle: beautiful things for real homes, made responsibly, priced fairly. There's a quiet confidence to the voice — it never shouts, never oversells, and trusts the product to speak for itself when described well enough.

## Voice Attributes

### 1. Sensory
- **What it means:** CozyNest copy makes you feel textures, see colors, and experience products through language. Abstract adjectives ("luxurious," "premium") are replaced with specific, tactile descriptions.
- **DO:** "Stone-washed Belgian linen that feels like a favorite t-shirt from day one. No stiff break-in period. Just soft."
- **DON'T:** "Our luxurious, premium-quality bedding provides an unparalleled sleep experience."

### 2. Honest
- **What it means:** CozyNest explains exactly what you're getting, why it costs what it costs, and what makes the material choice matter. No vague "sustainable" claims — specific certifications, specific practices.
- **DO:** "Our linen is OEKO-TEX certified and woven at a family-run mill in Portugal that's been making textiles since 1962. The flax is grown in Belgium — it's the best linen flax in the world, and it costs more. That's why our sheets are $148, not $48."
- **DON'T:** "Sustainably sourced, eco-friendly bedding for the conscious consumer."

### 3. Relatable
- **What it means:** CozyNest is for people who live in their homes — who have kids, pets, coffee spills, and laundry piles. The brand isn't aspirational in a museum-catalog way. It's aspirational in a "my home feels genuinely good" way.
- **DO:** "These sheets survive the washing machine, the dog, and Sunday morning pancake crumbs. They actually get softer every wash."
- **DON'T:** "Transform your bedroom into a five-star hotel sanctuary."

### 4. Understated
- **What it means:** CozyNest doesn't yell. No all-caps, no "BEST EVER," no exclamation points in headlines. The brand is confident enough to be quiet.
- **DO:** "Good sheets. Made well. That's all we do."
- **DON'T:** "THE BEST SHEETS YOU'LL EVER OWN! You NEED These!!!"

## Engagement Context

**When CopyDTC started:** CozyNest had beautiful products and strong photography but struggled to communicate why their sheets cost $148 when big-box retailers sell sheets for $40. Their copy defaulted to generic luxury language ("premium," "luxurious," "elevated") that sounded exactly like every other home brand. Email was minimal — a single welcome discount email and occasional sales blasts. Instagram was gorgeous images with one-line captions.

**What we built:** Complete content engine — Brand Voice Bible, 5-email welcome sequence, abandoned cart flow, seasonal campaign emails, 6 product description rewrites, homepage copy rewrite, "Why Linen" educational landing page, Meta ad copy for 3 campaigns, and Instagram content calendar with story-driven captions.

**Timeline:** Full system delivered in 16 days.
