## Key Results — First 90 Days

### Email Marketing

| Metric | Before CopyDTC | After 90 Days | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Email revenue as % of total** | 8% | 24% | +200% |
| **Welcome sequence revenue/recipient** | $0.28 | $2.42 | +764% |
| **Welcome sequence open rate** | 32% | 54% | +69% |
| **Welcome sequence click rate** | 1.2% | 5.1% | +325% |
| **Abandoned cart recovery rate** | 2.4% | 7.8% | +225% |
| **Average order value (email channel)** | $118 | $156 | +32% |

**What drove this:** CozyNest's previous welcome email was a single message: "Welcome! Here's 15% off." No story, no education, no brand building. Customers either used the discount or didn't — and the brand trained them to wait for sales.

The new 5-email sequence delays the discount to Email 5 and spends Emails 1–4 educating on linen vs. cotton, helping with color selection, and sharing care instructions. This builds brand affinity and product understanding BEFORE any purchase pressure. Result: 68% of welcome-sequence purchasers bought at full price (up from 22%).

The abandoned cart sequence addresses the #1 objection for premium home goods — price — with a "cost over time" comparison that reframes $148 as cheaper than replacing $40 cotton sheets every year. Recovery rate jumped from 2.4% to 7.8%.

AOV increased 32% because the welcome sequence educates on the full product line (sheets, duvet cover, pillowcases) and positions the complete set as the best value, rather than letting customers default to the cheapest single item.

### Website Conversion

| Metric | Before CopyDTC | After 90 Days | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Homepage conversion rate** | 1.2% | 2.8% | +133% |
| **Product page conversion rate** | 2.0% | 3.4% | +70% |
| **"Why Linen" page conversion rate** | N/A (new page) | 4.1% | New |
| **Average time on product page** | 42 seconds | 1 min 38 sec | +133% |
| **Bounce rate (overall)** | 54% | 38% | -30% |

**What drove this:** The homepage copy shifted from aspirational-generic ("Elevate Your Sleep Experience") to specific-sensory ("Sheets That Get Better Every Year You Own Them"). The new copy leads with the unique linen benefit (gets softer over time) instead of generic luxury claims.

The "Why Linen" educational landing page became the highest-converting page on the site. We now route all "awareness" traffic — blog referrals, social content, top-of-funnel ads — through this page instead of sending cold traffic directly to product pages. Visitors who read the linen vs. cotton comparison convert at 4.1% vs. 3.4% on the standard product page. Education drives conversion.

Product descriptions were rewritten from feature lists to sensory, narrative copy. Each product page now includes a "what it feels like" section with specific, tactile language — because you can't touch sheets through a screen, so the copy has to do the touching for you.

### Paid Advertising

| Metric | Before CopyDTC | After 90 Days | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta CTR** | 0.9% | 2.6% | +189% |
| **Meta CPA** | $52 | $31 | -40% |
| **Meta ROAS** | 1.6x | 3.2x | +100% |
| **Pinterest CTR** | 0.4% | 1.1% | +175% |
| **Pinterest saves rate** | 2.1% | 5.8% | +176% |

**What drove this:** Previous ads used generic home-goods language ("Premium bedding for your dream bedroom"). New ads led with the counterintuitive hook: "Your sheets peaked the day you bought them" and "Your sheets are made from the same fabric as a $12 t-shirt." These hooks stop scrollers by challenging an assumption they've never questioned.

The "cost over time" ad angle ($148 once vs. $400 over 5 years) consistently outperformed the "quality" angle by 35% on CTR. Price objection isn't about the number — it's about the perceived value per year.

Pinterest became a significant channel with targeted "linen bedroom" and "sustainable home" pins. Saves rate nearly tripled, creating a long-tail traffic source that continues generating visits months after posting.

### Social Media

| Metric | Before CopyDTC | After 90 Days | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram engagement rate** | 1.6% | 3.9% | +144% |
| **Average caption length** | 8 words | 125 words | +1,463% |
| **Instagram-attributed revenue** | $800/mo | $3,600/mo | +350% |
| **Pinterest monthly views** | 12K | 68K | +467% |

**What drove this:** Instagram captions went from generic one-liners ("New color just dropped. Link in bio.") to story-driven, sensory content. Posts about the linen softening process, the Portuguese mill, and "cotton vs. linen" education consistently outperform product-only posts by 3x on engagement.

The key insight: CozyNest's audience doesn't want to be sold to. They want to be informed and inspired. Educational content ("Why linen gets softer over time" / "How to pick your sheet color based on your bedroom") drives significantly more engagement than promotional content ("Sale ends Sunday!").
