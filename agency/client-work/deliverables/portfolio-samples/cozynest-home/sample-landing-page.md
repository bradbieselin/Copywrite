### Section 5: Care Guide

**Headline:** Easy to Care For. Built to Last.

**Washing:** Machine wash cold or warm with gentle detergent. No special care needed.

**Drying:** Low heat or air dry. High heat is the only thing that shortens linen's lifespan. We recommend tumble dry on low for 10 minutes, then hang or lay flat.

**Ironing:** Don't. The natural texture is the point. Linen is meant to look relaxed, not pressed.

**Tip:** Wash before first use. Your sheets are already stone-washed, but the first home wash removes residual sizing and kickstarts the softening process. You'll notice the difference after one cycle.

**The bottom line:** Treat them like regular sheets. Skip the dry cleaner, skip the specialty detergent, skip the ironing board. Just wash and enjoy. They get better over time regardless.

**Why do these cost $148 when I can buy sheets for $40?**
Two reasons: material and construction. The Belgian flax in our sheets costs 4–5x more per yard than the cotton used in most sheet sets. The stone-washing process adds another production step. And the Portuguese mill we work with pays living wages and operates under EU labor standards. The $40 sheets save money by using cheaper fiber, overseas production, and fabric that wears out in a year. Our sheets cost more upfront but less over time — $148 for 10–15 years vs. $40 every year.

**Are they scratchy? I've heard linen can be rough.**
Not ours. We stone-wash every set before shipping, which pre-softens the fabric and skips the stiff break-in period that gives linen a bad reputation. They feel soft and lived-in from the first night. And they continue getting softer with every wash.

**Do they wrinkle?**
Yes. Beautifully. Linen's natural texture includes gentle wrinkles — that's the "lived-in" look that people love about linen bedding. If you want military-corner crispness, linen isn't for you. If you want your bed to look effortlessly inviting, it's perfect.

**What if I don't like them?**

**Are these sustainable?**
Specifically sustainable, not vaguely sustainable. Flax (the plant linen comes from) uses 4x less water than cotton. Our flax is rain-fed in Belgium — no irrigation needed. The fabric is OEKO-TEX certified (tested for 100+ harmful substances). Dyes are GOTS-certified natural. The mill runs on 60% renewable energy. And because the sheets last 10–15 years, you're not throwing away and replacing textiles every year. The most sustainable product is the one you don't have to replace.
