## Key Results — First 90 Days

### Email Marketing

| Metric | Before CopyDTC | After 90 Days | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Email revenue as % of total** | 11% | 28% | +154% |
| **Welcome sequence revenue/recipient** | $0.42 | $2.18 | +419% |
| **Welcome sequence open rate** | 38% | 52% | +37% |
| **Welcome sequence click rate** | 1.8% | 6.4% | +256% |
| **Abandoned cart recovery rate** | 3.2% | 8.7% | +172% |
| **Post-purchase repeat rate (60-day)** | 14% | 23% | +64% |

**What drove this:** The original welcome sequence was a single email with a discount code. We replaced it with a 5-email sequence that builds brand affinity before offering any discount. Emails 1–4 tell the brand story, educate on ingredients, showcase social proof, and build a routine around the customer's skin type. The discount comes in email 5 — and by that point, 62% of customers had already purchased at full price.

The abandoned cart flow went from a single "you forgot something" email to a 3-email sequence that handles objections (skin type compatibility, timeline to results, money-back guarantee) and only introduces free shipping as an incentive in the final email.

### Website Conversion

| Metric | Before CopyDTC | After 90 Days | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Product page conversion rate** | 2.1% | 3.8% | +81% |
| **Average time on product page** | 48 seconds | 1 min 52 sec | +133% |
| **Homepage bounce rate** | 62% | 41% | -34% |
| **Radiance Serum page conversion** | 2.4% | 5.1% | +113% |

**What drove this:** Product descriptions were rewritten from feature-led ("Contains 15% L-ascorbic acid, hyaluronic acid, vitamin E, and ferulic acid") to benefit-led copy with an ingredient transparency angle. The Radiance Serum landing page was rebuilt with a pain-problem-solution structure, competitive comparison table, clinical results, and a full-transparency ingredient breakdown. Time on page nearly doubled, indicating visitors were actually reading the copy — not just scanning and bouncing.

### Paid Advertising

| Metric | Before CopyDTC | After 90 Days | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta CTR** | 1.2% | 3.4% | +183% |
| **Meta CPA** | $38 | $22 | -42% |
| **Meta ROAS** | 2.1x | 3.8x | +81% |
| **Google Search CTR** | 2.8% | 4.6% | +64% |

**What drove this:** Previous ad copy was generic ("Clean skincare for beautiful skin"). New ad copy led with a differentiated hook: ingredient transparency and honest concentrations. The "Most vitamin C serums are lying to you" angle consistently outperformed benefit-only messaging by 40–60%. Testing 3 hook variations per campaign (pain point, social proof, benefit-led) allowed rapid identification of winners across audiences.
