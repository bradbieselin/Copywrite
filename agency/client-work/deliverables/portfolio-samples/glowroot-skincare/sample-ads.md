## Creative Direction Notes

**For the Meta ads:**
- **Static images:** Product bottle with a clean, bright background. Overlay text showing the key stat (e.g., "15% Vitamin C" in bold). Consider a split-image showing a generic serum label (blurred ingredients) vs. GlowRoot's label (clear percentages).
- **Video (15s):** Open with a hand flipping a competitor bottle to show the vague ingredient list (1–2 seconds). Cut to GlowRoot bottle with clear percentages highlighted. Voiceover: "Most vitamin C serums won't show you this. We will." End on the product with the key stat on screen.
- **UGC-style:** Customer holding the serum, talking to camera about their results. Keep it unpolished — real lighting, real skin, no filters. Authenticity outperforms production value for this audience.
