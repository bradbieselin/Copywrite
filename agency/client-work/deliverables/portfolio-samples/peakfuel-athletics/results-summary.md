## Key Results — First 90 Days

### Email Marketing

| Metric | Before CopyDTC | After 90 Days | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Email revenue as % of total** | 9% | 26% | +189% |
| **Welcome sequence revenue/recipient** | $0.31 | $1.94 | +526% |
| **Welcome sequence open rate** | 34% | 58% | +71% |
| **Welcome sequence click rate** | 1.4% | 5.8% | +314% |
| **Abandoned cart recovery rate** | 2.8% | 9.2% | +229% |
| **Post-purchase repeat rate (60-day)** | 18% | 31% | +72% |

**What drove this:** PeakFuel's original welcome email was a single message with a discount code and product grid. We replaced it with a 5-email educational sequence that positions the brand as the honest alternative in a dishonest industry. The "proprietary blend" exposé email (Email 2) has a 62% open rate — the highest in the entire flow — and drives significant brand affinity before any purchase pressure.

The post-purchase cross-sell sequence was new. By timing the cross-sell recommendation 10 days after delivery (when the customer has experienced the product), we achieved a 14% click rate on the cross-sell email — 4x higher than industry average.

### Website Conversion

| Metric | Before CopyDTC | After 90 Days | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Product page conversion rate** | 1.8% | 3.6% | +100% |
| **Average time on product page** | 38 seconds | 2 min 14 sec | +253% |
| **Comparison page conversion rate** | N/A (new page) | 4.8% | New |
| **Homepage bounce rate** | 58% | 39% | -33% |

**What drove this:** The product comparison landing page — showing PeakFuel against major competitors across transparency, dosing, testing, and price — became the highest-converting page on the site. Visitors who hit the comparison page convert at 4.8% vs. 3.6% on the standard product page. We now direct all "awareness" traffic (blog, social, top-of-funnel ads) to this page.

Product descriptions shifted from ingredient lists to a narrative structure: problem (what's wrong with the industry) → solution (how PeakFuel is different) → proof (lab results + reviews) → action. Average time on page increased 253%, indicating visitors are reading, not bouncing.

### Paid Advertising

| Metric | Before CopyDTC | After 90 Days | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta CTR** | 1.0% | 2.8% | +180% |
| **Meta CPA** | $42 | $24 | -43% |
| **Meta ROAS** | 1.8x | 3.4x | +89% |
| **Google Search CTR** | 3.1% | 5.2% | +68% |
| **TikTok video views (organic)** | 200 avg | 8,400 avg | +4,100% |

**What drove this:** The pre-CopyDTC ad strategy was feature-focused ("26g protein, great taste, fast recovery"). The new strategy led with industry exposé hooks — "Your protein is probably amino spiked," "Proprietary blends exist to hide bad dosing" — that stopped scrollers and positioned PeakFuel as the honest alternative. The transparency angle outperformed benefit-only messaging by 65% on CTR.

TikTok was the breakout channel. The "label flip" script concept (comparing competitor proprietary blend labels to PeakFuel's open label) generated 3 videos with 25K+ views each. One hit 142K views organically. These weren't polished productions — they were filmed in a gym with an iPhone. Authenticity drove reach.
