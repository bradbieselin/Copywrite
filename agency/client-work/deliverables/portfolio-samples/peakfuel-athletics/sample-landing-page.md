The supplement industry has a math problem. Here's how it works:

A brand puts "25g Protein" on the front of the label. On the back, buried in a proprietary blend, they've added cheap amino acids — glycine, taurine, creatine — that technically register as "protein" on a nitrogen test but don't contribute to muscle protein synthesis.

This is called **amino spiking.** It's legal. It's common. And it means you're paying for protein you're not getting.
