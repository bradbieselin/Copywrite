## Creative Direction Notes

**For Meta ads:**
- **Static images:** Clean product shot on a gym/training background. No shredded models. Show the label clearly — the transparency angle is the visual hook. Consider a split image: competitor label (blurred proprietary blend) vs. PeakFuel label (clear dosages).
- **Video (15s):** The label flip concept works across all platforms. Keep it raw and unpolished — gym lighting, real hands, no studio production. Authenticity beats polish for this audience.
- **UGC-style:** Real athlete (not a sponsored influencer) talking about the product while mixing a shake at the gym. Focus on the transparency angle, not the taste/flavor angle.

**For TikTok:**
- Fast cuts, text overlays, direct-to-camera energy
- The "exposing the supplement industry" angle consistently performs well on TikTok
- Use trending sounds when relevant but don't force trend participation
- Educational content > lifestyle content for this brand
