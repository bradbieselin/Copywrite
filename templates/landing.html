FAQ
Questions you're probably asking.
How is this different from hiring a freelance copywriter?
Freelancers write individual pieces. We build content systems. You get a Brand Voice Bible, monthly content calendar, performance tracking, and a full pipeline of deliverables across every channel. It's a content engine, not a gig.
What kinds of DTC brands do you work with?
We work best with DTC brands doing $500K to $10M in annual revenue. That includes skincare, supplements, apparel, CPG, food and beverage, fitness, pet products, and home goods. If you sell direct to consumers online, we can help.
What does "20 deliverables" actually mean?
A deliverable is one piece of content: one email, one ad variation, one blog post, one landing page, one product description. Your monthly allocation is flexible. Want 10 emails and 10 ads? Done. Want 5 blog posts, 10 social posts, and 5 emails? Also done.
How fast do you deliver?
Standard turnaround is 3-5 business days for most deliverables. Rush turnaround (48 hours) is available on all plans. Blog posts and landing pages may take slightly longer due to research requirements.
Do you use AI to write the copy?
We use AI as a production tool, yes. But every piece goes through human review, brand voice verification, and quality checks before it reaches you. AI lets us deliver faster and more consistently. The strategy, voice calibration, and quality control are human.
What if I'm not happy with the copy?
Every deliverable includes two rounds of revisions. On the Scale plan, revisions are unlimited. We also do a Brand Voice Bible calibration in your first week so we get the tone right from the start. Most clients approve first drafts without changes.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment?
We work on a month-to-month basis. No long-term contracts. We recommend at least 3 months to see compounding results, but you're free to cancel anytime with 30 days notice.
What's included in the free content audit?
We review your current emails, ads, landing pages, and product copy. You get a scorecard rating each channel, specific recommendations for improvement, and estimated revenue impact. It's a real audit with real insights, not a sales pitch disguised as value.
