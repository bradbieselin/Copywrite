Brad Bieselin

I write copy that makes people stop scrolling and start buying.

I got into copywriting the same way most people do — by accident. I was helping a friend rewrite the product descriptions for their Shopify store, and within a week their conversion rate had nearly doubled. That was the moment I realized words weren't just words. They were the difference between a brand people scroll past and one they buy from without hesitation.

Since then, I've focused almost entirely on DTC brands — the kind building something real in a crowded market. Email sequences, ad copy, landing pages, social media — I've written it all, and I've learned what actually moves the needle versus what just fills a content calendar.

My approach is simple: I spend more time understanding your customer than I do writing. The best copy doesn't feel like marketing — it feels like someone finally said exactly what your buyer was already thinking. That's what I aim for in everything I write.

When I'm not writing, I'm studying what other brands are doing, pulling apart ads that stopped my own scroll, and figuring out why certain hooks work and others don't. This is the kind of thing I do for fun — which probably means I'm in the right industry.

If you've got a brand that deserves better copy, I'd love to talk.
